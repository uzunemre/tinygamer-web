Privacy Policy

Pixnora: Photo Cleaner · Last updated: 2026-11-22

Summary

This policy explains what information the Pixnora: Photo Cleaner app ("the App") handles. The App is operated by Emre Uzun ("we", "us").

  • Your photos and videos never leave your iPhone. Everything the App does with them (browsing, finding similar photos, sizing videos) happens on your device. We never upload, copy or see your photos.
  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We don't run our own servers or analytics, and we don't sell your data.
  • Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. Purchases are handled by Apple and verified through RevenueCat.

1. Photo library access

The App asks for access to your photo library so you can review and delete photos and videos. It reads photos, videos and their basic details (date, size, duration, type) on your device to show them and to group similar photos. Nothing is deleted until you confirm in the iOS system dialog, and deleted items stay in the Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album for 30 days. You can change or revoke access anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

2. Data stored on your device

The App saves on your device only: your settings (language, sort order, haptics), your progress (which items you chose to keep, which similar groups you reviewed) and simple totals (how many items you deleted, how much space you freed). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you use the free version, the App shows banner ads and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (for example, ad views and clicks, crashes, performance).

This data is used to deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. Ads never have access to your photos. Pixnora Pro removes all ads. See how Google uses data from apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have Pixnora Pro, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information (such as iOS version and country). See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

5. Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: iOS asks whether the App may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalized with your advertising identifier. Change it anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): where required, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown. You can change your choice later in the App under Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Limit ad personalization: iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Sharing and retention

We do not share personal data with anyone other than what Google AdMob and RevenueCat receive as described above. We keep no personal data on our side. Google and RevenueCat retain data according to their own policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we don't hold personal data about you, requests about ad data can be made through Google's privacy controls; you can also contact us with any question.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with the date above.
